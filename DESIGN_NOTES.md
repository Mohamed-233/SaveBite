# SaveBite visual assets

- `assets/savebite-logo.svg`: code-native wordmark with a bowl and leaf symbol.
- `assets/savebite-mark.svg`: compact mark for the collapsed navigation.
- `assets/theme.css`: responsive presentation, button hover/focus/press states and reduced-motion support.
- `assets/food-sharing.png`: generated editorial-style hero illustration/photo. This is a visual asset, not documentation of a real donation or partner.

The hero was produced with the built-in image generation tool and copied into the project's assets folder. It is included in the ZIP so the app does not depend on an external image URL.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: website hero photograph for SaveBite, a food rescue web application connecting restaurants with charities. Create a polished natural editorial food photo, wide landscape composition approximately 3:2. On a clean pale warm stone countertop, neat recyclable kraft food donation boxes containing fresh rice, cooked vegetables and salad, with a few fresh tomatoes and leafy greens nearby. Two natural human hands gently passing one closed food box at the edge of frame, no faces. Warm soft daylight, realistic appetizing food textures, balanced sophisticated composition, muted forest-green and cream visual palette, subtle background depth, premium community food-service website photography. No text, labels, logos, watermarks, UI, badges or captions. Focus on food and the act of sharing. Leave some calm empty countertop space around the food, avoid clutter. This photo will be cropped inside a rounded web hero card.

The design changes presentation only. Algorithm details remain in developer documentation, while the public route view shows the destination, road distance and route. Dashed access connectors retain a short label so they are not confused with verified roads.
